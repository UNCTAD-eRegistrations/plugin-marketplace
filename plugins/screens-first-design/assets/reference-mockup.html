1. The list, by state

Rows with icon chip, key figure, and a status badge: Draft (amber) / To complete (blue) / Confirmed (green). One color language for the whole product.

2. Confirm from the detail

"Confirm…" opens the confirm sheet in the same page: exact recap of what happens, one-breath warning, slide to confirm. Try it: the band and the badge flip to "Confirmed".

3. The pieces to copy

Phone frame (overflow:clip), title bar + "?" help, status badges, bottom sheet + backdrop + Escape, slide-to-confirm (pointer events), floating pill (nowrap), 860/440px media queries.
